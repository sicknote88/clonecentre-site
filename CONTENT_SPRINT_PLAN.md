## Positioning

**"The AI guy in Liverpool."** Nobody owns this. Say it in every bio, every intro, every video:

- TikTok / IG / YT bio: `Liverpool's AI guy. I show normal people & local businesses how to actually use AI. Free guide 👇`
- Sign-off catchphrase idea: "That's the craic with AI — la." (own the Scouse directness; it's the differentiator against generic American AI bros)
- The soul ID avatar does the talking-head delivery; you record screen demos for the "show me" moments. Face + voice stay consistent across every video.

## The funnel (already built — use it)

```
Short video (hook = real search query)
  → bio link → joseph.clonecentre.ai/trends  (the map IS the lead magnet — shareable, no signup needed)
  → free Prompt Guidebook (email capture on homepage)
  → weekly group call (/community, founding-member price)
  → 1-to-1 AI Fix Session (Cal.com) / Pro community (Stripe)
```

## Cadence

- **2 short-form videos per day** (TikTok + IG Reels + YT Shorts — same file, all three).
- Each video = one real query from the map as the hook, 30–60 seconds, one clear answer, CTA: *"Full map's in my bio — it shows everything people are searching."*
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Comments on day 1–3 videos are your market research for the group call.

## The weekly refresh loop

Every Monday: `npm run trends` in `clonecentre_site/`, then redeploy. The map updates with the newest searches → pick the 3 biggest movers → they become next week's videos. When Google Trends GB flags an AI headline as "hot", that node pulses on the map — film a reaction video **same day**, news-jacking is the fastest viral lever you have.
