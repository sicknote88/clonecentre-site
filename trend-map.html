    WHERE THIS DATA COMES FROM

    No guesses, no guru opinions, no made-up numbers. Here's exactly how the map is built.

    1 · GOOGLE AUTOCOMPLETE

    The real suggestions Google shows when people type — generated from actual searches. We pull them for 42 AI seed phrases and keep the 671 unique queries people genuinely search.

    2 · YOUTUBE SUGGEST

    The same mechanism on YouTube — what viewers search before they watch. If a query shows up on both Google and YouTube, it ranks higher on the map.

    3 · GOOGLE TRENDS (UK)

    Daily trending searches for the UK flag which topics are in the news right now — those nodes pulse. 12-month interest timelines power the momentum score and sparkline on each cluster.

    REFRESHED, NOT STREAMED

    The map re-pulls everything from live sources on a weekly schedule and shows you the exact date it was last updated. Search behaviour moves weekly, not hourly — so what you see is current reality, not a stale blog post from 2023.
