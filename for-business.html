Before anyone pays

A PILOT IS A WRITTEN PAGE, NOT A VERBAL YES.

  • One workflow only. Missed calls, or website questions. Not every job in the business.
  • What the assistant may answer, and what always goes to a person.
  • Which systems are actually connected. A booking is not promised until that connection is checked.
  • How long the pilot runs. ANSAIR has no free trial, but the plan fee is refundable for 14 days. Anything different is agreed first.
  • How we will judge it, and the price after the pilot. That price is the public plan or a quote agreed before go-live.
Clinics, dental and aesthetics

ROUTINE QUESTIONS ONLY.

The assistant can take appointment enquiries, opening hours and “please ask a person to call me.” It does not diagnose, recommend a treatment, or say whether a procedure is suitable. Those questions are passed to a person, with the caller’s details, and nothing else.
